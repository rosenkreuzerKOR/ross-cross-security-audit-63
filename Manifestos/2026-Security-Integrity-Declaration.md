The 2026 Declaration of Security Integrity: The Principle of Conditional Mandate

I. Preamble
Inspired by the constitutional principles of James Otis—"No taxation without representation"—the Order of the Rose Cross declares that in an era of global strategic competition, "No Security, No Mandate." We will not entrust the sovereignty of the Republic of Korea to those who ignore the erosion of our national integrity.
     
II. The Mandate of the Sovereign
Representation is a conditional agreement between the voter and the representative. Any political entity that fails to protect the nation’s core security assets and its most vital alliance partners (USFK) forfeits its right to be the recipient of our collective vote.
     
III. Core Requirements (The 4 Pillars)
- Physical Security: Legislative barriers against malign foreign capital acquiring land near strategic military and energy assets.
- Academic Integrity: Purging the "Silent Invasion" from universities and protecting intellectual property from state-affiliated actors.
- Administrative Transparency: Exposing foreign government lobbying and funding through a localized FARA framework.
- Digital De-risking: Ensuring that public infrastructure is free from high-risk foreign surveillance technology.
     
IV. Final Resolution
Until the official adoption of these pillars into the 2026 Local Election platforms, our mandate remains withheld. We are not merely observers; we are the auditors of our national security.
